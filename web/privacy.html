Privacy Policy

FC Song Manager · last updated 9 November 2026

FC Song Manager is a private tool used by one studio owner to manage backing-track mixdowns for a small group of vocalists. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses in Google Drive

The app requests only the drive.file permission. With this permission it can see and manage only the files it creates itself. It cannot read, list or change any other file in the Google Drive account.

  • The app uploads final WAV files of songs that a vocalist has accepted, so they can be delivered to the recipients.
  • These WAV files are temporary. The app deletes them from Google Drive after delivery, following the studio's cleanup rules. The original files stay on the studio computer.

What data is stored

  • Song titles, dates, prices, payment status and delivery status, stored in the app's database.
  • Phone numbers of recipients, entered by the studio owner, used only to deliver the files.

Sharing

Data and files are not sold or shared with third parties. Files are sent only to the recipients of each song (the First Club operator and the vocalist who owns the song).

Credentials

Google access credentials are stored only on the studio computer and in the app's private server configuration. They are never sent to the web pages that vocalists open.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the studio owner through the Google account that operates this app.
